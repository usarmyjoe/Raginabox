# Backups

## The 3-2-1 rule
Keep 3 copies of your data, on 2 different media, with 1 copy offsite.

## Restore testing
A backup you have never restored is a hope, not a backup. Restore a random set of files every quarter and time it.

## Retention
Keep daily snapshots for 14 days, weekly for 8 weeks, monthly for 12 months. Verify pruning with a dry run first.
